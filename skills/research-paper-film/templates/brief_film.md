# Brief template: research film

**Paper:** <title>, <venue year>, DOI <doi>
**Target:** ~<60 | 120> s · 3840×2160 · 60 fps · 16:9 · narration <yes/no, language>
**Audience:** <domain experts + technical newcomers>

## The viewer must understand
1. **Problem:** why <the space / task> is hard, and why prior approaches fall short.
2. **Idea:** what <method> does differently, shown as a mechanism, not a name.
3. **Impact:** the strongest verified results, with their conditions.

## Non-negotiable
- Every structure, number and claim comes from the paper or from a re-implementation checked against it.
- Conceptual visuals are labelled as conceptual.
- No invented metrics, baselines or conclusions; units and conditions travel with the numbers.
- Speech is never time-stretched. The audio is licence-clean.
- Do not claim to have rendered, reviewed or checked anything that was not actually done.

## Acts (refine as needed)
I Challenge · II Idea · III Inside the algorithm (centrepiece) · IV Impact · V Title (title, venue, DOI, credit)

## Deliverables
- Master (.mov) and distribution MP4s (4K, 1080p)
- SRT; audio stems; storyboard and timing sheet
- Verification notes; QA reviews
- Source and reproduce instructions
