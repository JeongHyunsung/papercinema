# 07: QA

Every QA document starts by saying who did the review. For example: "Reviews were carried out by an independent reviewer agent (a fresh context each round that saw only the delivered video, its subtitles, the paper and its own earlier reviews; references/18) and by the user on rounds 2, 4 and 6. Audits marked *self-check* were run by the generator. Each finding is tied to re-runnable evidence."

The audits below are the generator's self-checks before each handoff. The reviewer's own measurements come from `scripts/review_pack.py` (references/19).

## Readability audit (`scripts/audit.py`)

- **How it works.** The script instruments `Canvas.text` and steps the film clock at 20 fps.
- **What it records.** For each distinct string, the longest continuous run at ≥ 50% opacity, and when that run starts.
- **Budgets:**

  | Kind of text | Readable for at least |
  |---|---|
  | label / number | 2.5 s |
  | headline result | 3–4 s |
  | reasoning (two lines that must be compared) | 4–7 s |
- **Process:**
  1. Run the audit on the old cut and the new cut.
  2. Publish a table: label, old time and duration, new time and duration.
  3. For each string under budget, do one of three things: fix it (lengthen the hold in the EDL), remove it (the narration or the step marker carries it), or justify it (e.g. a live counter that changes every step on purpose).
- **Counts to report:** strings under budget before, fixed, removed, remaining.

## Contact sheets

- **Delivered film:** one frame per second, tiled 6×N with timestamps. Inspect them all.
- **Typical catches:**
  - text crossing arcs or other labels;
  - clipping at frame edges;
  - empty beats;
  - glows leaving grey ghosts on white;
  - a legend colliding with a data label;
  - a title overlapping an axis.

## Motion and linkage audits (references/14)

- **`motion_audit.py preview.mp4 --acts "I:0-29,…" --holds 123-129 --json motion.json`**, then `gate.py --motion motion.json --centre III`.
  - Report static %, longest static run, camera %, camera moves and mean change, for the film and per act.
  - Open the per-second list and fix every static run longer than 3 s outside declared holds.
- **`linkage_audit.py film --from <Act II> --to <end of Act III> --allow "<HUD readouts>"`.**
  - Every finding shown unanchored for ≥ 0.6 s is a FAIL: pin it with `callout_at` or move it into the HUD.
- **One viewing at 1× with sound**, noting the time of every moment where nothing happens or a label's referent is unclear.
  - Contact sheets and stills cannot show these.
- Run the motion audit again on the delivered file.

## Audio QA

- **`mix_check.py`:** ASR of each narration clip cut from `mix.wav` vs from `narration.wav`. Report the word difference.
- **`srt_check.py`:** ASR of the delivered file's own audio inside each cue window vs the cue text.
  - Normalize numbers and respellings before comparing.
  - Report the total word differences.
  - Inspect every cue that differs: is it an ASR error or a real mismatch?
- **`ebur128`:** run it on every deliverable.

## Reviews (the reviewer's `review.md` files, references/19)

The QA document (`deliver/docs/QA.md`, written by the orchestrator from the findings ledger in `LOOP.md`) collects the loop: one row per finding (`F<round>.<n>`) across all rounds, with its severity, source (reviewer or human), the round it opened and closed, and its end state (fixed / closed by ruling / open). The reviewer's lenses are the old three reviews, now applied from outside: scientist (every number against the paper, with the arithmetic shown), motion designer (identity, rhythm, slow moments, runtime, sound separation), first-time viewer (problem, idea and impact in one sentence each), plus skeptic, coverage and peaks.

List known limitations as **open**, with a reason. Never mark something as fixed without a re-run check.
